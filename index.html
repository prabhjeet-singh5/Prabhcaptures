About

Hi, I'm Prabh.

I picked up a camera professionally in 2022 and haven't put it down since. What started with weddings around Ludhiana now takes me to Chandigarh, Haryana, Delhi NCR and destination shoots across India — a lot of pheras, haldi chaos and 4am baraat calls later.

My approach is simple: stay out of the way. The best frames are the ones nobody posed for — the laugh mid-sentence, the hand squeeze before the pheras, the cousin crying in the back row. Light setup, minimal direction, honest edit.

Every booking is handled personally, from the first call to the final album in your hands. No handing your wedding off to a stranger with a camera.
